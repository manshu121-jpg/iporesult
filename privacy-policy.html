Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

IPOCheck is an independent IPO allotment-status interface. It is not SEBI, NSE, BSE, or an IPO registrar.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, the website may process information such as your PAN, selected IPO, and technical information required to operate the website.

2. PAN Storage

By default, IPOCheck does not intentionally save your PAN in persistent browser storage. If you switch on the optional “Save PAN” consent, the PAN may be stored locally on your device/browser to make future use easier. You can switch the option off or use the Delete Saved PAN option at any time.

3. Use of Information

Information is intended to be used only for providing the requested IPO-status functionality, security, troubleshooting, and service improvement where applicable.

4. Third-Party / Official Sources

Where IPO status is obtained through an official or authorized registrar/source, that source may have its own privacy policy and terms. Users should review those policies before submitting personal information.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect personal information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Deletion

For PAN saved locally through the website, use “Delete Saved PAN”. Clearing browser/site data may also remove locally stored information.

7. Children

This service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when the website, technology, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, users should contact the website operator using the contact details published on the website.
